# FDA Food Code 2022 4-402.11 Fixed Equipment, Spacing or Sealing

## 4-402.11(A)
(A) EQUIPMENT that is fixed because it is not EASILY MOVABLE shall be
installed so that it is: (1) Spaced to allow access for cleaning along the
sides, behind, and above the EQUIPMENT; (2) Spaced from adjoining EQUIPMENT,
walls, and ceilings a distance of not more than 1 millimeter or one thirty-
second inch; or (3) SEALED to adjoining EQUIPMENT or walls, if the EQUIPMENT
is exposed to spillage or seepage.

## 4-402.11(B)
(B) COUNTER-MOUNTED EQUIPMENT that is not EASILY MOVABLE shall be installed to
allow cleaning of the EQUIPMENT and areas underneath and around the EQUIPMENT
by being: (1) SEALED; or (2) Elevated on legs as specified under ¶
4-402.12(D).
