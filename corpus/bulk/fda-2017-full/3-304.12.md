## 3-304.12
During pauses in FOOD preparation or dispensing, FOOD preparation and
dispensing UTENSILS shall be stored: (A) Except as specified under ¶ (B) of
this section, in the FOOD with their handles above the top of the FOOD and the
container;

## 3-304.12(B)
(B) In FOOD that is not TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD with their
handles above the top of the FOOD within containers or EQUIPMENT that can be
closed, such as bins of sugar, flour, or cinnamon;

## 3-304.12(C)
(C) On a clean portion of the FOOD preparation table or cooking EQUIPMENT only
if the in-use UTENSIL and the FOOD-CONTACT surface of the FOOD preparation
table or cooking EQUIPMENT are cleaned and SANITIZED at a frequency specified
under §§ 4-602.11 and 4-702.11;

## 3-304.12(D)
(D) In running water of sufficient velocity to flush particulates to the
drain, if used with moist FOOD such as ice cream or mashed potatoes;

## 3-304.12(E)
(E) In a clean, protected location if the UTENSILS, such as ice scoops, are
used only with a FOOD that is not TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD; or

## 3-304.12(F)
(F) In a container of water if the water is maintained at a temperature of at
least 57°C (135°F) and the container is cleaned at a frequency specified under
Subparagraph 4­ 602.11(D)(7).
