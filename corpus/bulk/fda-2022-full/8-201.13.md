## 8-201.13(A)
(A) Before engaging in an activity that requires a HACCP PLAN, a PERMIT
applicant or PERMIT HOLDER shall submit to the REGULATORY AUTHORITY for
approval a properly prepared HACCP PLAN as specified under § 8-201.14 and the
relevant provisions of this Code if: (1) Submission of a HACCP PLAN is
required according to LAW; (2) A VARIANCE is required as specified under
Subparagraph 3- 401.11(D)(4), § 3-502.11, or ¶ 4-204.110(B); (3) The
REGULATORY AUTHORITY determines that a FOOD preparation or processing method
requires a VARIANCE based on a plan submittal specified under § 8-201.12, an
inspectional finding, or a VARIANCE request.

## 8-201.13(B)
(B) Before engaging in REDUCED OXYGEN PACKAGING without a VARIANCE as
specified under § 3-502.12, a PERMIT applicant or PERMIT HOLDER shall submit a
properly prepared HACCP PLAN to the REGULATORY AUTHORITY.
