## 4-203.13
Equipment. Pressure measuring devices that display the pressures in the water
supply line for the fresh hot water SANITIZING rinse shall have increments of
7 kilopascals (1 pound per square inch) or smaller and shall be accurate to
±14 kilopascals (±2 pounds per square inch) in the range indicated on the
manufacturer’s data plate. 4-204 Functionality
