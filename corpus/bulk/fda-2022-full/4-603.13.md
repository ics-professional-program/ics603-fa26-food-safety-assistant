## 4-603.13
Soiled items to be cleaned in a WAREWASHING machine shall be loaded into
racks, trays, or baskets or onto conveyors in a position that: (A) Exposes the
items to the unobstructed spray from all cycles; and (B) Allows the items to
drain.
