## 4-602.11(A)
(A) Equipment food-contact surfaces and utensils shall be cleaned: (1) Except
as specified in ¶ (B) of this section, before each use with a different type
of raw animal FOOD such as beef, FISH, lamb, pork, or POULTRY; P (2) Each time
there is a change from working with raw FOODS to working with READY-TO-EAT
FOODS; P (3) Between uses with raw fruits and vegetables and with
TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD; P (4) Before using or storing a FOOD
TEMPERATURE MEASURING DEVICE; P and (5) At any time during the operation when
contamination may have occurred. P
