# FDA Food Code 2022 6-202.11 Light Bulbs, Protective Shielding

## 6-202.11(A)
(A) Except as specified in ¶ (B) of this section, light bulbs shall be
shielded, coated, or otherwise shatter-resistant in areas where there is
exposed FOOD; clean EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, and LINENS; or unwrapped SINGLE-
SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES.

## 6-202.11(B)
(B) Shielded, coated, or otherwise shatter-resistant bulbs need not be used in
areas used only for storing FOOD in unopened packages, if: (1) The integrity
of the packages cannot be affected by broken glass falling onto them; and (2)
The packages are capable of being cleaned of debris from broken bulbs before
the packages are opened.

## 6-202.11(C)
(C) An infrared or other heat lamp shall be protected against breakage by a
shield surrounding and extending beyond the bulb so that only the face of the
bulb is exposed.
