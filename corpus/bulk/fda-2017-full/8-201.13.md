## 8-201.13(A)
(A) B efore engaging in an activity that requires a HACCP PLAN, a PERMIT
applicant or PERMIT HOLDER shall submit to the REGULATORY AUTHORITY for
approval a properly prepared HACCP PL AN as specified under § 8-201.14 and the
relevant provisions of this Code if: (1) Submission of a HACCP PLAN is
required according to LAW; (2 ) A VARIANCE is required as specified under
Subparagraph 3-401.11(D)(4), § 3-502.11, or ¶ 4-204.110(B); (3) The REGULATORY
AUTHORITY determines that a FOOD preparation or processing method requires a
VARIANCE based on a plan submittal specified under § 8-201.12, an inspectional
finding, or a VARIANCE request.
