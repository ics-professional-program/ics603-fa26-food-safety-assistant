## 6-501.12(A)
(A) PHYSICAL FACILITIES shall be cleaned as often as necessary to keep them
clean. (B) Except for cleaning that is necessary due to a spill or other
accident, cleaning shall be done during periods when the least amount of FOOD
is exposed such as after closing.
