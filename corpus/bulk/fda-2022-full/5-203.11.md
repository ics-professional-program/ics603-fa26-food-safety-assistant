## 5-203.11(A)
(A) Except as specified in ¶¶ (B) of this section, at least 1 HANDWASHING
SINK, a number of HANDWASHING SINKS necessary for their convenient use by
EMPLOYEES in areas specified under § 5-204.11, and not fewer than the number
of HANDWASHING SINKS required by LAW shall be provided. Pf

## 5-203.11(B)
(B) If APPROVED and capable of removing the types of soils encountered in the
FOOD operations involved, automatic handwashing facilities may be substituted
for HANDWASHING SINKS in a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT that has at least 1 HANDWASHING
SINK.
