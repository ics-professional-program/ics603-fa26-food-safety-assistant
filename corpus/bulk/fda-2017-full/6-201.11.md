## 6-201.11
Except as specified under § 6-201.14 and except for antislip floor coverings
or applications that may be used for safety reasons, floors, floor coverings,
walls, wall coverings, and ceilings shall be designed, constructed, and
installed so they are SMOOTH and EASILY CLEANABLE.
