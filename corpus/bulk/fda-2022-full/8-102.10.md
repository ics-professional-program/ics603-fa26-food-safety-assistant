## 8-102.10
Addressed. (A) If necessary to protect against public health HAZARDS or
nuisances, the REGULATORY AUTHORITY may impose specific requirements in
addition to the requirements contained in this Code that are authorized by
LAW.

## 8-102.10(B)
(B) The REGULATORY AUTHORITY shall document the conditions that necessitate
the imposition of additional requirements and the underlying public health
rationale. The documentation shall be provided to the PERMIT applicant or
PERMIT HOLDER and a copy shall be maintained in the REGULATORY AUTHORITY’S
file for the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT. 8-103 Variances
