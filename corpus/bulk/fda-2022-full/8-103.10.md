## 8-103.10
The REGULATORY AUTHORITY may grant a VARIANCE by modifying or waiving the
requirements of this Code if in the opinion of the REGULATORY AUTHORITY a
health HAZARD or nuisance will not result from the VARIANCE. If a VARIANCE is
granted, the REGULATORY AUTHORITY shall retain the information specified under
§ 8-103.11 in its records for the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT.
