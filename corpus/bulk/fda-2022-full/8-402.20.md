## 8-402.20
Access. If a person denies access to the regulatory authority, the regulatory
authority shall: (A) Inform the PERSON that: (1) The permit holder is required
to allow access to the regulatory authority as specified under § 8-402.11 of
this Code, (2) Access is a condition of the acceptance and retention of a food
establishment permit to operate as specified under ¶ 8-304.11(F), and (3) If
access is denied, an order issued by the appropriate authority allowing
access, hereinafter referred to as an inspection order, may be obtained
according to LAW; and (B) Make a final request for access.
