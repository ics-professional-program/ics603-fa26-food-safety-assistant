# SOP-03 — Employee Illness Reporting (Pacific Market Cafe)

Company policy on when sick employees must stay out of the kitchen.

## Symptoms requiring exclusion
Employees experiencing vomiting, diarrhea, or jaundice must report it to the
manager on duty and may not work with food. Employees with vomiting or
diarrhea may return only after being symptom-free for at least 48 hours.

## Reportable diagnoses
A diagnosis of Norovirus, Salmonella, Shigella, E. coli (STEC), or
Hepatitis A must be reported to the manager, who reports it to the local
health department. These employees are excluded from the establishment.

## Return to work
Return to work after a reportable diagnosis requires manager approval and,
where required by the health department, written medical clearance.
