## 3-501.12
Frozen TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD that is slacked to moderate
the temperature shall be held: (A) Under refrigeration that maintains the FOOD
temperature at  5°C (41°F) or less; or (B) At any temperature if the FOOD
remains frozen.
