## 5-501.16
Availability. (A) An inside storage room and area and outside storage area and
enclosure, and receptacles shall be of sufficient capacity to hold REFUSE,
recyclables, and returnables that accumulate.

## 5-501.16(B)
(B) A receptacle shall be provided in each area of the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT or
PREMISES where REFUSE is generated or commonly discarded, or where recyclables
or returnables are placed. (C) If disposable towels are used at handwashing
lavatories, a waste receptacle shall be located at each lavatory or group of
adjacent lavatories.
