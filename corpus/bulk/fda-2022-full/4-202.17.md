## 4-202.17
Kick plates shall be designed so that the areas behind them are accessible for
inspection and cleaning by being: (A) Removable by one of the methods
specified under Subparagraph 4-202.11(A)(5) or capable of being rotated open;
and (B) Removable or capable of being rotated open without unlocking EQUIPMENT
doors.
