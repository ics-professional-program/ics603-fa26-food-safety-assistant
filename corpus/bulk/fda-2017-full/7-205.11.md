## 7-205.11
Lubricants shall meet the requirements specified in 21 CFR 178.3570 Lubricants
with incidental food contact, if they are used on FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES, on
bearings and gears located on or within FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES, or on bearings
and gears that are located so that lubricants may leak, drip, or be forced
into FOOD or onto FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES. P Pesticides
