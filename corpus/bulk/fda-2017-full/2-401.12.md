## 2-401.12
FOOD EMPLOYEES experiencing persistent sneezing, coughing, or a runny nose
that causes discharges from the eyes, nose, or mouth may not work with exposed
FOOD; clean EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, and LINENS; or unwrapped SINGLE-SERVICE or
SINGLE-USE ARTICLES.
