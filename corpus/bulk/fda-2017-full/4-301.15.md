## 4-301.15(A)
(A) Except as specified in ¶ (B) of this section, if work clothes or LINENS
are laundered on the PREMISES, a mechanical clothes washer and dryer shall be
provided and used.

## 4-301.15(B)
(B) If on-PREMISES laundering is limited to wiping cloths intended to be used
moist, or wiping cloths are air-dried as specified under § 4-901.12, a
mechanical clothes washer and dryer need not be provided. Utensils,
