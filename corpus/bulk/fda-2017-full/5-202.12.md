## 5-202.12(A)
(A) A HANDWASHING SINK shall be equipped to provide water at a temperature of
at least 38°C (100°F) through a mixing valve or combination faucet. Pf

## 5-202.12(B)
(B) A steam mixing valve may not be used at a HANDWASHING SINK. (C) A self-
closing, slow-closing, or metering faucet shall provide a flow of water for at
least 15 seconds without the need to reactivate the faucet. (D) An automatic
handwashing facility shall be installed in accordance with manufacturer’s
instructions.
