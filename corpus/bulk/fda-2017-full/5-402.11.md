## 5-402.11(A)
(A) Except as specified in ¶¶ (B), (C), and (D) of this section, a direct
connection may not exist between the SEWAGE system and a drain originating

## 5-402.11(B)
(B) Paragraph (A) of this section does not apply to floor drains that
originate in refrigerated spaces that are constructed as an integral part of
the building.
