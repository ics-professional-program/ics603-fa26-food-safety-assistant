## 4-204.122
Apparatuses, such as dollies, pallets, racks, and skids used to store and
transport large quantities of PACKAGED FOODS received from a supplier in a
cased or overwrapped lot, shall be  designed to be moved by hand or by
conveniently available apparatuses such as hand trucks and forklifts.
