## 4-602.12(A)
(A) The FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES of cooking and baking EQUIPMENT shall be cleaned
at least every 24 hours. This section does not apply to hot oil cooking and
filtering EQUIPMENT if it is cleaned as specified in Subparagraph
4-602.11(D)(6). (B) The cavities and door seals of microwave ovens shall be
cleaned at least every 24 hours by using the manufacturer's recommended
cleaning procedure.
