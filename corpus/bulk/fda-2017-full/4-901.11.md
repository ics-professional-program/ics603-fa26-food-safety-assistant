## 4-901.11
After cleaning and SANITIZING, EQUIPMENT and UTENSILS: (A) Shall be air-dried
or used after adequate draining as specified in the first paragraph of 40 CFR
180.940 Tolerance exemptions for active and inert ingredients for use in
antimicrobial formulations (food-contact surface SANITIZING solutions), before
contact with FOOD; and (B) May not be cloth dried except that UTENSILS that
have been air-dried may be polished with cloths that are maintained clean and
dry.
