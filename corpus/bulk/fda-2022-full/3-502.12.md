## 3-502.12(A)
(A) Except for a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT that obtains a VARIANCE as specified under
§ 3-502.11, a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT that PACKAGES TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR
SAFETY FOOD using a REDUCED OXYGEN PACKAGING method shall control the growth
and toxin formation of Clostridium botulinum and the growth of Listeria
monocytogenes. P

## 3-502.12(C)
