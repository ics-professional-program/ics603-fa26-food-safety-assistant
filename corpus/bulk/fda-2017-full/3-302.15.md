## 3-302.15(A)
(A) Except as specified in ¶ (B) of this section and except for whole, raw
fruits and vegetables that are intended for washing by the CONSUMER before
consumption, raw fruits and vegetables shall be thoroughly washed in water to
remove soil and other contaminants before being cut, combined with other
ingredients, cooked, served, or offered for human consumption in READY-TO-EAT
form.

## 3-302.15(B)
(B) Fruits and vegetables may be washed by using chemicals as specified under
§ 7-204.12. (C) Devices used for on-site generation of chemicals meeting the
requirements specified in 21 CFR 173.315, Chemicals used in the washing or to
assist in the peeling of fruits and vegetables, for the washing of raw, whole
fruits and vegetables shall be used in accordance with the manufacturer’s
