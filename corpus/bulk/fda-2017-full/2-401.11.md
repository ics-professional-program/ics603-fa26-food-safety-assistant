## 2-401.11(A)
(A) Except as specified in ¶ (B) of this section, an EMPLOYEE shall eat,
drink, or use any form of tobacco only in designated areas where the
contamination of exposed FOOD; clean EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, and LINENS;
unwrapped SINGLE-SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES; or other items needing
protection can not result.

## 2-401.11(B)
(B) A FOOD EMPLOYEE may drink from a closed BEVERAGE container if the
container is handled to prevent contamination of: (1) The EMPLOYEE'S hands;
(2) The container; and (3) Exposed FOOD; clean EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, and
LINENS; and unwrapped SINGLE-SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES.
