# FDA Food Code 2017 §1-201.10 — Major Food Allergens

Adapted from the FDA Food Code 2017 edition, definition of "major food
allergen" (§1-201.10). This is the edition that preceded the 2022 Food
Code. Teaching data, not current guidance.

## The eight major food allergens
The 2017 edition defines eight major food allergens: milk, eggs, fish
(such as bass, flounder, and cod, including crustacean shellfish such as
crab, lobster, and shrimp), tree nuts (such as almonds, pecans, and
walnuts), wheat, peanuts, and soybeans. Sesame is not among the major
food allergens defined by this edition; it was added later, as the ninth
allergen, in the 2022 edition. A food ingredient that contains protein
derived from one of the listed foods is also a major food allergen.

## What is not a major food allergen
Highly refined oils derived from a listed food, and ingredients derived
from such oils, are not major food allergens. Ingredients exempted under
the Food Allergen Labeling and Consumer Protection Act petition process
are also excluded.
