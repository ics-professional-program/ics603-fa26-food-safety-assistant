## 3-202.17
When received by a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT, SHELLSTOCK shall be reasonably free of
mud, dead SHELLSTOCK, and SHELLSTOCK with broken shells.  Dead SHELLSTOCK with
badly broken shells shall be discarded. Packaging and Identification
