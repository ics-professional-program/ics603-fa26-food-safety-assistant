## 6-403.11(A)
(A) Areas designated for EMPLOYEES to eat, drink, and use TOBACCO PRODUCTS
shall be located so that FOOD, EQUIPMENT, LINENS, and SINGLE-SERVICE and
SINGLE-USE ARTICLES are protected from contamination.

## 6-403.11(B)
(B) Lockers or other suitable facilities shall be located in a designated room
or area where contamination of FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS, and SINGLE-
