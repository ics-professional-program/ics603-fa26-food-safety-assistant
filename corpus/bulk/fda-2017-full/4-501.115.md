## 4-501.115
Sanitization Using Detergent-Sanitizers. If a detergent-SANITIZER is used to
SANITIZE in a cleaning and SANITIZING procedure where there is no distinct
water rinse between the washing and SANITIZING steps, the agent applied in the
SANITIZING step shall be the same detergent-SANITIZER that is used in the
washing step.
