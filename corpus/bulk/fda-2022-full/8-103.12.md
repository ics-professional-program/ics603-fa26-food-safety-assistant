## 8-103.12
If the REGULATORY AUTHORITY grants a VARIANCE as specified in § 8-103.10, or a

HOLDER shall:

## 8-103.12(A)
(A) Maintain the approved variance at the food establishment; Pf and (B)
Comply with the HACCP plans and procedures that are submitted as specified
