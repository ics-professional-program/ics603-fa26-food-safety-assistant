## 6-201.13(A)
(A) In FOOD ESTABLISHMENTS in which cleaning methods other than water flushing
are used for cleaning floors, the floor and wall junctures shall be coved and
closed to no larger than 1 mm (one thirty-second inch).

## 6-201.13(B)
(B) The floors in FOOD ESTABLISHMENTS in which water flush cleaning methods
are used shall be provided with drains and be graded to drain, and the floor
and wall junctures shall be coved and SEALED.
