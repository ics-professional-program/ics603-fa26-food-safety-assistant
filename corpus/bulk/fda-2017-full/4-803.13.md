## 4-803.13(A)
(A) Except as specified in ¶ (B) of this section, laundry facilities on the
PREMISES of a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT shall be used only for the washing and drying
of items used in the operation of the establishment.

## 4-803.13(B)

general laundering such as for institutions providing boarding and lodging may
