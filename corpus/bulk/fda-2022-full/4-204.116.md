## 4-204.116
If hot water is used for SANITIZATION in manual WAREWASHING operations, the
SANITIZING compartment of the sink shall be: (A) Designed with an integral
heating device that is capable of maintaining water at a temperature not less
than 77°C (171°F); Pf and (B) Provided with a rack or basket to allow complete
immersion of equipment and utensils into the hot water. Pf
