## 5-502.12
REFUSE, recyclables, and returnables shall be removed from the PREMISES by way
of: (A) Portable receptacles that are constructed and maintained according to
LAW; or (B) A transport vehicle that is constructed, maintained, and operated
according to LAW. Facilities for Disposal and Recycling
