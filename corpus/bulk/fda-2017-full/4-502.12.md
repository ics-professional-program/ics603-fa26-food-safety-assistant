## 4-502.12
Use. A FOOD ESTABLISHMENT without facilities specified under Parts 4-6 and 4-7
for cleaning and SANITIZING KITCHENWARE and TABLEWARE shall provide only
SINGLE-USE KITCHENWARE, SINGLE-SERVICE ARTICLES, and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES for
use by FOOD EMPLOYEES AND PSINGLE-SERVICE ARTICLES for use by CONSUMERS.
