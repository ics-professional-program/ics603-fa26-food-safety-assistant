## 3-401.12
Raw animal FOODS cooked in a microwave oven shall be: (A) Rotated or stirred
throughout or midway during cooking to compensate for uneven distribution of
heat;

## 3-401.12(B)
(B) Covered to retain surface moisture; (C) Heated to a temperature of at
least 74°C (165°F) in all parts of the FOOD;P and (D) Allowed to stand covered
for 2 minutes after cooking to obtain temperature equilibrium.
