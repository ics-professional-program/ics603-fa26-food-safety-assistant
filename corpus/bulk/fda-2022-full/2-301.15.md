## 2-301.15
FOOD EMPLOYEES shall clean their hands in a HANDWASHING SINK or APPROVED
automatic handwashing facility and may not clean their hands in a sink used
for FOOD preparation or WAREWASHING, or in a service sink or a curbed cleaning
facility used for the disposal of mop water and similar liquid waste. Pf
