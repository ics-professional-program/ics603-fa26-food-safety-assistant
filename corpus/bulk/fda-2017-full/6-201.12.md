## 6-201.12(A)
(A) Utility service lines and pipes may not be unnecessarily exposed. (B)
Exposed utility service lines and pipes shall be installed so they do not
obstruct or prevent cleaning of the floors, walls, or ceilings. (C) Exposed
horizontal utility service lines and pipes may not be installed on the floor.
