## 5-302.16
A hose used for conveying DRINKING WATER from a water tank shall be: (A) Safe;
P (B) Durable, CORROSION-RESISTANT, and nonabsorbent; (C) Resistant to
pitting, chipping, crazing, scratching, scoring, distortion, and
decomposition; (D) Finished with a SMOOTH interior surface; and (E) Clearly
and durably identified as to its use if not permanently attached. Numbers and
