## 4-501.11(A)
(A) EQUIPMENT shall be maintained in a state of repair and condition that
meets the requirements specified under Parts 4-1 and 4-2. (B) EQUIPMENT
components such as doors, seals, hinges, fasteners, and kick plates shall be
kept intact, tight, and adjusted in accordance with manufacturer's
specifications.

## 4-501.11(C)
(C) Cutting or piercing parts of can openers shall be kept sharp to minimize
the creation of metal fragments that can contaminate FOOD when the container
is opened.
