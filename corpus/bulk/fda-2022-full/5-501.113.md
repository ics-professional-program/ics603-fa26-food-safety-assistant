## 5-501.113
Receptacles and waste handling units for REFUSE, recyclables, and returnables
shall be kept covered: (A) Inside the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT if the receptacles
and units: (1) Contain FOOD residue and are not in continuous use; or (2)
After they are filled; and (B) With tight-fitting lids or doors if kept
outside the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT.
