## 8-403.40
The regulatory authority shall: (A) Inform a PERSON who declines to sign an
acknowledgment of receipt of inspectional findings as specified in § 8-403.30
that: (1) An acknowledgment of receipt is not an agreement with findings, (2)
Refusal to sign an acknowledgment of receipt will not affect the PERMIT
HOLDER'S obligation to correct the violations noted in the inspection report
within the time frames specified, and (3) A refusal to sign an acknowledgment
of receipt is noted in the inspection report and conveyed to the REGULATORY
AUTHORITY'S historical record for the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT; and (B) Make a final
request that the PERSON IN CHARGE sign an acknowledgment receipt of
inspectional findings.
