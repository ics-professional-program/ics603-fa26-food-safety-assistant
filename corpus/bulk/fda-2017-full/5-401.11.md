## 5-401.11
A SEWAGE holding tank in a mobile FOOD ESTABLISHMENT shall be: (A) Sized 15
percent larger in capacity than the water supply tank; and (B) Sloped to a
drain that is 25 mm (1 inch) in inner diameter or greater, equipped with a
