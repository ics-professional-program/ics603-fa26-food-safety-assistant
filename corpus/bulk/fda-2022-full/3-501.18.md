## 3-501.18
Disposition. (A) A FOOD specified in ¶ 3-501.17(A) or (B) shall be discarded
if it: (1) Exceeds the temperature and time combination specified in ¶ 3-
501.17(A), except time that the product is frozen; P (2) Is in a container or
PACKAGE that does not bear a date or day; P or (3) Is inappropriately marked
with a date or day that exceeds a temperature and time combination as
specified in ¶ 3-501.17(A).P

## 3-501.18(B)
(B) Refrigerated, READY-TO-EAT, TIME/TEMPERATURE CONTROL FOR SAFETY FOOD
prepared in a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT and dispensed through a VENDING MACHINE with
an automatic shutoff control shall be discarded if it exceeds a temperature
and time combination as specified in ¶ 3-501.17(A). P
