## 5-103.12
Water under pressure shall be provided to all fixtures, EQUIPMENT, and nonFOOD
EQUIPMENT that are required to use water except that water supplied as
specified under ¶¶ 5-104.12(A) and (B) to a TEMPORARY FOOD ESTABLISHMENT or in
response to a temporary interruption of a water supply need not be under
pressure. Pf Distribution, Delivery, and Retention
