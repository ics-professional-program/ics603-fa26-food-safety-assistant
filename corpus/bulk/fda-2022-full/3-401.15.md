## 3-401.15(A)
(A) Commercially PACKAGED FOOD that bears a manufacturer’s cooking
instructions shall be cooked according to those instructions before use in
READY- TO-EAT FOODS or offered in unPACKAGED form for human consumption,
unless the manufacturer’s instructions specify that the FOOD may be consumed
without cooking. P

## 3-401.15(B)
(B) FOOD for which the manufacturer has provided information that it has not
been processed to control pathogens, when used in READY-TO-EAT FOODS or
offered for human consumption, shall be cooked according to a time and
temperature appropriate for the FOOD. P 3-402 Freezing
