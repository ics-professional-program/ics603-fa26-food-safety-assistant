## 6-301.12
Each HANDWASHING SINK or group of adjacent HANDWASHING SINKS shall be provided
with: (A) Individual, disposable towels; Pf (B) A continuous towel system that
supplies the user with a clean towel; Pf or ( C) A heated-air hand drying
device; Pf or ( D) A hand drying device that employs an air-knife system that
delivers high velocity, pressurized air at ambient temperatures. Pf
