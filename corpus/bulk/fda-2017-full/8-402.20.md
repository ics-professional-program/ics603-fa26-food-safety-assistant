## 8-402.20
Final Request for Access. If a PERSON denies access to the REGULATORY
AUTHORITY, the REGULATORY AUTHORITY shall: (A) Inform the PERSON that: (1) The
PERMIT HOLDER is required to allow access to the REGULATORY AUTHORITY as
specified under § 8-402.11 of this Code,
Report of Findings (2) Access is a condition of the acceptance and retention
of a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT PERMIT to operate as specified under ¶ 8-304.11(F),
and (3) If access is denied, an order issued by the appropriate authority
allowing access, hereinafter referred to as an inspection order, may be
obtained according to LAW; and (B) Make a final request for access.
