## 4-101.12(A)
(A) Except as specified in ¶¶ (B) and (C) of this section, cast iron may not
be used for UTENSILS or FOOD-CONTACT SURFACES of EQUIPMENT.

## 4-101.12(B)
(B) Cast iron may be used as a surface for cooking. (C) Cast iron may be used
in UTENSILS for serving FOOD if the UTENSILS are used only as part of an
uninterrupted process from cooking through service.
