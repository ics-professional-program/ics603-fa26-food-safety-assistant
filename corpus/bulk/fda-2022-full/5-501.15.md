## 5-501.15(A)
(A) Receptacles and waste handling units for REFUSE, recyclables, and
returnables used with materials containing FOOD residue and used outside the
FOOD ESTABLISHMENT shall be designed and constructed to have tight-fitting
lids, doors, or covers.

## 5-501.15(B)
(B) Receptacles and waste handling units for REFUSE and recyclables such as an
on-site compactor shall be installed so that accumulation of debris and insect
and rodent attraction and harborage are minimized and effective cleaning is
