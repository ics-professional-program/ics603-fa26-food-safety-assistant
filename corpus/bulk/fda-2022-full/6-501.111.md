## 6-501.111
The PREMISES shall be maintained free of insects, rodents, and other pests.
The presence of insects, rodents, and other pests shall be controlled to
eliminate their presence on the PREMISES by:

## 6-501.111(A)
(A) Routinely inspecting incoming shipments of FOOD and supplies; (B)
Routinely inspecting the PREMISES for evidence of pests; (C) Using methods, if
pests are found, such as trapping devices or other means of pest control as
specified under §§ 7-202.12, 7-206.12, and 7-206.13; Pf and (D) Eliminating
harborage conditions.
