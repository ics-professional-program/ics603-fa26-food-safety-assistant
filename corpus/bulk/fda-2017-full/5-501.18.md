## 5-501.18(A)
(A) Except as specified in ¶ (B) of this section, suitable cleaning implements
and supplies such as high pressure pumps, hot water, steam, and detergent
shall be provided as necessary for effective cleaning of receptacles and waste
handling units for REFUSE, recyclables, and returnables. (B) If APPROVED, off-
PREMISES-based cleaning services may be used if on-PREMISES cleaning
