# FDA Food Code §5-202.12 — Handwashing Sink Requirements

Adapted from the FDA Food Code 2022 edition, §§5-202.12 and 5-205.11.
Teaching data, not current guidance.

## Water temperature at handwashing sinks
A handwashing sink must be equipped to provide water at a temperature of at
least 85°F (29.4°C) through a mixing valve or combination faucet. A steam
mixing valve may not be used at a handwashing sink, because steam valves are
hard to control and can scald.

## Faucets
A self-closing, slow-closing, or metering faucet must provide a flow of
water for at least 15 seconds without the need to reactivate the faucet. An
automatic handwashing facility must be installed in accordance with the
manufacturer's instructions.

## Using handwashing sinks
A handwashing sink must be maintained so it is accessible at all times for
employee use, and may be used only for washing hands. It may not be used
for purposes such as rinsing utensils or dumping beverages.
