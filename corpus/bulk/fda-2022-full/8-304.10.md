## 8-304.10(A)
(A) At the time a PERMIT is first issued, the REGULATORY AUTHORITY shall
provide to the PERMIT HOLDER a copy of this Code so that the PERMIT HOLDER is
notified of the compliance requirements and the conditions of retention, as
specified under § 8-304.11, that are applicable to the PERMIT.

## 8-304.10(B)
(B) Failure to provide the information specified in ¶ (A) of this section does
not prevent the REGULATORY AUTHORITY from taking authorized action or seeking
remedies if the PERMIT HOLDER fails to comply with this Code or an order,
warning, or directive of the REGULATORY AUTHORITY.
