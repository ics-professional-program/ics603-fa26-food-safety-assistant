## 5-302.12
If a water tank is designed with an access port for inspection and cleaning,
the opening shall be in the top of the tank and:

## 5-302.12(A)
(A) Flanged upward at least 13 mm (one-half inch); and (B) Equipped with a
port cover assembly that is: (1) Provided with a gasket and a device for
securing the cover in place, and (2) Flanged to overlap the opening and sloped
to drain.
