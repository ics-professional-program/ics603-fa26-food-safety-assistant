# SOP-01 — Receiving and Storage (Pacific Market Cafe)

Company standard operating procedure for deliveries and dry/cold storage.

## Receiving temperatures
Check every refrigerated delivery with a calibrated probe thermometer.
Refrigerated TCS foods must arrive at 41°F (5°C) or below; frozen foods must
arrive frozen solid with no signs of thawing or refreezing. Reject any
delivery above temperature, with damaged packaging, or past its use-by date,
and record the rejection on the receiving log.

## Cold storage order
Store food in the walk-in from top shelf to bottom shelf in this order:
ready-to-eat foods, whole cuts of beef and pork, ground meats, and raw
poultry on the bottom shelf. Never store raw animal foods above
ready-to-eat foods.

## FIFO rotation
Use first-in, first-out (FIFO) rotation. Label every container with the
product name and date received or prepared; place newer stock behind older
stock.
