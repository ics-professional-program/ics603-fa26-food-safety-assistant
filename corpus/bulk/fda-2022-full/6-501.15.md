## 6-501.15
FOOD preparation sinks, HANDWASHING SINKS, and WAREWASHING EQUIPMENT may not
be used for the cleaning of maintenance tools, the preparation or holding of
maintenance materials, or the disposal of mop water and similar liquid wastes.
