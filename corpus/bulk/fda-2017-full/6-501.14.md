## 6-501.14
Discharge Prohibition. (A) Intake and exhaust air ducts shall be cleaned and
filters changed so they are not a source of contamination by dust, dirt, and
other materials. (B) If vented to the outside, ventilation systems may not
create a public health HAZARD or nuisance or unLAWful discharge.
