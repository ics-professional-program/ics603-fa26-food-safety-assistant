## 4-903.11(B)
(B) Clean EQUIPMENT and UTENSILS shall be stored as specified under ¶ (A) of
this section and shall be stored: (1) In a self-draining position that allows
air drying; and (2) Covered or inverted.

## 4-903.11(C)
(C) SINGLE-SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES shall be stored as specified under
¶ (A) of this section and shall be kept in the original protective PACKAGE or
stored by using other means that afford protection from contamination until
used.

## 4-903.11(D)
(D) Items that are kept in closed PACKAGES may be stored less than 15 cm (6
inches) above the floor on dollies, pallets, racks, and skids that are
designed as specified under § 4-204.122.
