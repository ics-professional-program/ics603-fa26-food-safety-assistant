# Hawaii HAR §11-50-61 — Handwashing Sink Installation

Adapted from Hawaii Administrative Rules Title 11, Chapter 50 (Food Safety
Code), §11-50-61(c), as amended effective August 2025. Teaching data, not
current guidance.

## Handwashing sink installation
Under Hawaii's Food Safety Code, a handwashing sink, if provided with hot
water, must be equipped with a mixing valve or combination faucet. A steam
mixing valve may not be used at a handwashing sink. A self-closing,
slow-closing, or metering faucet must provide a flow of water for at least
fifteen seconds without the need to reactivate the faucet, and faucets must
provide a sufficient flow of water to permit proper washing and rinsing of
hands.

## No minimum water temperature
The Hawaii rule does not set a minimum water temperature for handwashing
sinks: hot water is optional, and the mixing-valve requirement applies only
if hot water is provided. This differs from the FDA model Food Code, which
requires water of at least 85°F at a handwashing sink in its 2022 edition.
In Hawaii establishments, the state rule is the one the health inspector
enforces.
