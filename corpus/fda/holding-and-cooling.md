# FDA Food Code §3-501 — Holding and Cooling

Adapted excerpt on time/temperature control for safety (TCS) foods.

## Cold holding
TCS foods held cold must be maintained at 41°F (5°C) or below. Examples
include cut leafy greens, cut tomatoes, cut melons, cooked rice, dairy, and
raw or cooked meats held for later service.

## Hot holding
TCS foods held hot must be maintained at 135°F (57°C) or above. Food that
falls below 135°F for more than 4 hours must be discarded.

## Cooling cooked food
Cooked TCS food, including cooked rice, must be cooled from 135°F to 70°F
(57°C to 21°C) within 2 hours, and from 70°F to 41°F (21°C to 5°C) within an
additional 4 hours — 6 hours total. Use shallow pans, ice baths, or ice
paddles to speed cooling.

## Date marking
Ready-to-eat TCS food held longer than 24 hours must be date-marked and
discarded after 7 days when held at 41°F or below (day of preparation counts
as day 1).
