## 4-204.115
Devices. A WAREWASHING machine shall be equipped with a TEMPERATURE MEASURING
DEVICE that indicates the temperature of the water: (A) In each wash and rinse
tank; Pf and (B) As the water enters the hot water SANITIZING final rinse
manifold or in the chemical SANITIZING solution tank. Pf
