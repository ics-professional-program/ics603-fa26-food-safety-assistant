# FDA Food Code §4-501 — Cleaning and Sanitizing

Adapted from the FDA Food Code 2022 edition, §§4-501.114, 4-602.11, and
3-304.14: sanitization of equipment and utensils. Teaching data, not
current guidance.

## Sanitizer concentrations
Chlorine (bleach) sanitizing solutions must be maintained at 50–100 ppm with
at least 7 seconds of contact time. Quaternary ammonium (quat) sanitizers
are used per the manufacturer's label, commonly 200 ppm, with 30 seconds of
contact time. Test strips must be used to verify concentration.

## Food-contact surfaces
Food-contact surfaces and utensils in continuous use with TCS food must be
cleaned and sanitized at least every 4 hours. Surfaces must be washed,
rinsed, and sanitized — in that order — after each use with raw animal food.

## Wiping cloths
In-use wet wiping cloths must be stored in sanitizer solution at the proper
concentration between uses, never left on counters.
