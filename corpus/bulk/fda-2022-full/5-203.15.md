## 5-203.15(A)
(A) If not provided with an air gap as specified under §5-202.13, a dual check
valve with an intermediate vent preceded by a screen of not less than 100 mesh
to 25.4 mm (100 mesh to 1 inch) shall be installed upstream from a carbonating
device and downstream from any copper in the water supply line.P

## 5-203.15(B)
(B) A dual check valve attached to the carbonator need not be of the vented
type if an air gap or vented backflow prevention device has been otherwise
provided as specified under ¶ (A) of this section. 5-204 Location and
Placement
