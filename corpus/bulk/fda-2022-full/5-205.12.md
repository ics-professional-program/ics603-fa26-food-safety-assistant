## 5-205.12(A)
(A) A PERSON may not create a cross connection by connecting a pipe or conduit
between the DRINKING WATER system and a nonDRINKING WATER system or a water
system of unknown quality.P

## 5-205.12(B)
(B) The piping of a nonDRINKING WATER system shall be durably identified so
that it is readily distinguishable from piping that carries DRINKING WATER. Pf
