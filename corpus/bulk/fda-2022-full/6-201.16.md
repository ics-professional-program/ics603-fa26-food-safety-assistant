## 6-201.16(A)
(A) Wall and ceiling covering materials shall be attached so that they are
EASILY CLEANABLE. (B) Except in areas used only for dry storage, concrete,
porous blocks, or bricks used for indoor wall construction shall be finished
and SEALED to provide a SMOOTH, nonabsorbent, EASILY CLEANABLE surface.
