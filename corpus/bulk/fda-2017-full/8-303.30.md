## 8-303.30
If an application for a PERMIT to operate is denied, the REGULATORY AUTHORITY
shall provide the applicant with a notice that includes: (A) The specific
reasons and Code citations for the PERMIT denial;

## 8-303.30(B)
(B) The actions, if any, that the applicant must take to qualify for a PERMIT;
and (C) Advisement of the applicant's right of appeal and the process and time
frames for appeal that are provided in LAW.
