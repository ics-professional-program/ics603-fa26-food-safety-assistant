## 3-201.13
Fluid milk and milk products shall be obtained from sources that comply with
GRADE A STANDARDS as specified in LAW. P 3-201.14  Fish. (A) FISH that are
received for sale or service shall be: (1) Commercially and legally caught or
harvested; P or (2) APPROVED for sale or service. P (B) MOLLUSCAN SHELLFISH
that are recreationally caught may not be received for sale or service. P
