## 8-302.13
Applicants. To qualify for a PERMIT, an applicant shall: (A) Be an owner of
the FOOD ESTABLISHMENT or an officer of the legal ownership; (B) Comply with
the requirements of this Code;

## 8-302.13(C)
(C) As specified under § 8-402.11, agree to allow access to the FOOD
ESTABLISHMENT and to provide required information; and (D) Pay the applicable
PERMIT fees at the time the application is submitted.
