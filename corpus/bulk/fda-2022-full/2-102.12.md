## 2-102.12(A)
(A) The PERSON IN CHARGE shall be a certified FOOD protection manager who has
shown proficiency of required information through passing a test that is part
of an ACCREDITED PROGRAM.

## 2-102.12(B)
(B) This section does not apply to certain types of FOOD ESTABLISHMENTS deemed
by the REGULATORY AUTHORITY to pose minimal risk of causing, or contributing
to, foodborne illness based on the nature of the operation and extent of FOOD
preparation.
