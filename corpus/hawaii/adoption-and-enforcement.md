# Hawaii HAR Chapter 11-50 — The State Food Safety Code

Adapted from Hawaii Administrative Rules Title 11, Chapter 50 (Food Safety
Code), §§11-50-8 and 11-50-9, as amended effective August 2025. Teaching
data, not current guidance.

## What the Food Safety Code is
Hawaii Administrative Rules Chapter 11-50 is the state's food safety code,
administered by the Hawaii Department of Health. It is based on the FDA
model Food Code — most recently aligned with the 2022 edition through
amendments effective August 2025 — but it is its own rule, with
Hawaii-specific provisions and numbering. Where the state rule and the FDA
model code differ, the state rule is what applies to food establishments
in Hawaii.

## Inspections and placarding
Upon completion of an inspection, the department may post a color-coded
placard at the food establishment indicating its compliance status. The
placard must be posted where it is clearly visible to the general public
and patrons entering the establishment — for example, in a window within
five feet of the main entrance. The placard may not be defaced, hidden,
removed, relocated, or copied.
