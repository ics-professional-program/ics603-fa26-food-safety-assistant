# SOP-12 — Allergen Handling (Pacific Market Cafe)

Company procedure for preventing allergen cross-contact.

## The Big 9 allergens
The nine major food allergens are milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish,
tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans, and sesame. All menu items containing
them are flagged in the recipe binder.

## Preventing cross-contact
Prepare allergen-free orders first, on a cleaned and sanitized surface,
using the purple-handled allergen utensil kit. Wash hands and change gloves
before starting an allergen-free order. Never use shared fryer oil for an
allergen-free order.

## Guest allergen questions
Never guess about ingredients. Check the recipe binder, and have the
manager on duty confirm the answer with the guest before the order is fired.
