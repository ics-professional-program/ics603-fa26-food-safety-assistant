## 5-205.11(A)
(A) A HANDWASHING SINK shall be maintained so that it is accessible at all
times for EMPLOYEE use. Pf (B) A HANDWASHING SINK may not be used for purposes
other than handwashing. Pf (C) An automatic handwashing facility shall be used
in accordance with manufacturer’s instructions. Pf
