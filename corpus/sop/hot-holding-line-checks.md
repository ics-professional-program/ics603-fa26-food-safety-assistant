# SOP-09 — Hot Holding and Line Checks (Pacific Market Cafe)

Company standard operating procedure for hot-held food on the service line.
Where this SOP is stricter than the FDA Food Code or the Hawaii Food Safety
Code, the SOP governs company operations.

## Company hot-holding standard
Hold all hot TCS foods at 140°F (60°C) or above on the line. This is
deliberately stricter than the regulatory minimum of 135°F (57°C): the
extra five degrees gives the line margin, so a brief dip during a rush is
caught by a line check before the food ever falls below the regulatory
limit.

## Line checks
Probe every hot-held pan at opening and then every 2 hours, and record each
reading on the line-check log. Any pan reading below 140°F but at or above
135°F must be reheated rapidly to 165°F (74°C) and returned to holding.
Any pan found below 135°F for an unknown length of time must be discarded
and the discard noted on the log.
