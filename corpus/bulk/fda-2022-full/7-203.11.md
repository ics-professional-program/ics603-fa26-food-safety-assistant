## 7-203.11
A container previously used to store POISONOUS OR TOXIC MATERIALS may not be
used to store, transport, or dispense FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS,
SINGLE- SERVICE or SINGLE-USE ARTICLES . P 7-204 Chemicals
