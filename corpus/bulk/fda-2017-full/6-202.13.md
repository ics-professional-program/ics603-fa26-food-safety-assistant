## 6-202.13(A)
(A) Insect control devices that are used to electrocute or stun flying insects
shall be designed to retain the insect within the device. (B) Insect control
devices shall be installed so that: (1) The devices are not located over a
FOOD preparation area; and (2) Dead insects and insect fragments are prevented
from being impelled onto or falling on exposed FOOD; clean EQUIPMENT,
UTENSILS, and LINENS; and unwrapped SINGLE-SERVICE and SINGLE-USE ARTICLES.
