## 4-501.17
When used for WAREWASHING, the wash compartment of a sink, mechanical
warewasher, or wash receptacle of alternative manual WAREWASHING EQUIPMENT as
specified in ¶ 4-301.12(C), shall contain a wash solution of soap, detergent,
acid cleaner, alkaline cleaner, degreaser, abrasive cleaner, or other cleaning
agent according to the cleaning agent manufacturer's label instructions. Pf
