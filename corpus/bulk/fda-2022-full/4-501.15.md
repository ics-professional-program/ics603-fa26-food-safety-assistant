## 4-501.15(A)
(A) A WAREWASHING machine and its auxiliary components shall be operated in
accordance with the machine's data plate and other manufacturer's
instructions. (B) A WAREWASHING machine's conveyor speed or automatic cycle
times shall be maintained accurately timed in accordance with manufacturer's
specifications.
