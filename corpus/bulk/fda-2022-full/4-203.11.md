## 4-203.11(A)
(A) FOOD TEMPERATURE MEASURING DEVICES that are scaled only in Celsius or
dually scaled in Celsius and Fahrenheit shall be accurate to ±1°C in the
intended range of use. Pf (B) FOOD TEMPERATURE MEASURING DEVICES that are
scaled only in Fahrenheit shall be accurate to ±2°F in the intended range of
use. Pf
