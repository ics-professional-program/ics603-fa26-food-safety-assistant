# FDA Food Code 2017 §5-202.12 — Handwashing Sink Requirements

Adapted from the FDA Food Code 2017 edition, §5-202.12. This is the
edition that preceded the 2022 Food Code. Teaching data, not current
guidance.

## Water temperature at handwashing sinks
Under the 2017 edition, a handwashing sink must be equipped to provide
water at a temperature of at least 100°F (38°C) through a mixing valve or
combination faucet. The 2022 edition later lowered this minimum to 85°F.
A steam mixing valve may not be used at a handwashing sink.

## Faucets
A self-closing, slow-closing, or metering faucet must provide a flow of
water for at least 15 seconds without the need to reactivate the faucet.
An automatic handwashing facility must be installed in accordance with the
manufacturer's instructions.
