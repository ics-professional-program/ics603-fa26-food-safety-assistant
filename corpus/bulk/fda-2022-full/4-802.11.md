## 4-802.11(A)
(A) LINENS that do not come in direct contact with FOOD shall be laundered
between operations if they become wet, sticky, or visibly soiled. (B) Cloth
gloves used as specified in ¶ 3-304.15(D) shall be laundered before being used
with a different type of raw animal FOOD such as beef, FISH, lamb, pork or
POULTRY.

## 4-802.11(C)
(C) LINENS that are used as specified under § 3-304.13 and cloth napkins shall
be laundered between each use. (D) Wet wiping cloths shall be laundered daily.
(E) Dry wiping cloths shall be laundered as necessary to prevent contamination
of FOOD and clean serving UTENSILS. 4-803 Methods
