## 6-501.110(A)
(A) Dressing rooms shall be used by EMPLOYEES if the EMPLOYEES regularly
change their clothes in the establishment. (B) Lockers or other suitable
facilities shall be used for the orderly storage of EMPLOYEE clothing and
other possessions.
