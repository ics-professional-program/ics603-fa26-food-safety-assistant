## 4-205.10
FOOD EQUIPMENT that is certified or classified for sanitation by an American
National Standards Institute (ANSI)-accredited
certification program is deemed to comply with Parts 4-1 and 4-2 of this
chapter. 4-3 NUMBERS AND CAPACITIES Subparts 4-301 Equipment 4-302 Utensils,
Temperature Measuring Devices, and Testing Devices 4-303 Cleaning Agents and
Sanitizers Equipment
