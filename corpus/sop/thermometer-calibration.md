# SOP-07 — Thermometer Calibration (Pacific Market Cafe)

Company procedure for keeping probe thermometers accurate.

## Calibration frequency
Calibrate every probe thermometer daily before opening, after it is dropped,
and after any large temperature swing (e.g., moving between the freezer and
the cook line).

## Ice-point method
Fill a container with crushed ice and cold water. Insert the probe at least
2 inches without touching the container and wait until the reading
stabilizes (about 1 minute). Adjust the thermometer to read 32°F (0°C).

## Records and tolerance
Record each calibration on the calibration log. A thermometer that cannot
be adjusted to within ±2°F of 32°F must be removed from service and
replaced.
