# SOP-03 — Employee Illness Reporting (Pacific Market Cafe)

The employee health policy that the Institute of Child Nutrition HACCP-based
standard operating procedure "Personal Hygiene" (2018) requires an
establishment to keep but does not supply, written here in the same SOP
format. Pacific Market Cafe is a fictional establishment; the 48-hour return
rule below is stricter than the Food Code and is the cafe's own policy.
Teaching data, not current guidance.

Purpose: to prevent an ill employee from transmitting foodborne illness to
food, to other employees, or to guests. Scope: this procedure applies to
every employee who handles, prepares, or serves food, and to the manager on
duty who receives their reports. The 48-hour return rule is a house standard
and is stricter than the Food Code, which allows an employee to be
reinstated after 24 symptom-free hours; the extra day covers the period in
which an employee can still shed the organism after symptoms stop. Where
this SOP is stricter than the code, the SOP governs company operations.

## Symptoms requiring exclusion
Employees experiencing vomiting, diarrhea, or jaundice must report it to the
manager on duty and may not work with food. Employees with vomiting or
diarrhea may return only after being symptom-free for at least 48 hours.

## Reportable diagnoses
A diagnosis of Norovirus, Salmonella, Shigella, E. coli (STEC), or
Hepatitis A must be reported to the manager, who reports it to the local
health department. These employees are excluded from the establishment.

## Return to work
Return to work after a reportable diagnosis requires manager approval and,
where required by the health department, written medical clearance. Return
after vomiting or diarrhea without a diagnosis requires the full 48
symptom-free hours and the approval of the manager on duty.

## Corrective action
Send home any employee who reports or shows a symptom requiring exclusion,
and reassign their station. Discard any food they may have contaminated, and
clean and sanitize the surfaces and equipment they used. Retrain any
employee who worked a shift without reporting a symptom requiring exclusion.

## Verification and record keeping
The manager on duty records each illness report, the date the employee was
excluded, and the date they were cleared to return on the employee illness
log. Discarded food is recorded on the damaged or discarded product log. The
general manager reviews, initials, and dates the illness log weekly, and the
log is kept on file for a minimum of one year.
