## 4-203.12
Water. (A) Ambient air and water TEMPERATURE MEASURING DEVICES that are scaled
in Celsius or dually scaled in Celsius and Fahrenheit shall be designed to be
easily readable and accurate to ±1.5°C in the intended range of use. Pf

## 4-203.12(B)
(B) Ambient air and water TEMPERATURE MEASURING DEVICES that are scaled only
in Fahrenheit shall be accurate to ±3°F in the intended range of use. Pf
