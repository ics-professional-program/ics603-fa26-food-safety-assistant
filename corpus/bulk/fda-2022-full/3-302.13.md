## 3-302.13
Pasteurized EGGS or EGG PRODUCTS shall be substituted for raw EGGS in the
preparation of FOODS such as Caesar salad, hollandaise or Béarnaise sauce,
mayonnaise, meringue, eggnog, ice cream, and EGG-fortified BEVERAGES that are
not: P (A) Cooked as specified under Subparagraphs 3-401.11(A)(1) or (2); P or
(B) Included in ¶ 3-401.11(D). P
