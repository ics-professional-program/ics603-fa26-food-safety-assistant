## 4-603.12(A)
(A) FOOD debris on EQUIPMENT and UTENSILS shall be scraped over a waste
disposal unit or garbage receptacle or shall be removed in a WAREWASHING
machine with a prewash cycle. (B) If necessary for effective cleaning,
UTENSILS and EQUIPMENT shall be preflushed, presoaked, or scrubbed with
abrasives.
