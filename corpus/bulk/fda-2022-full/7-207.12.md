## 7-207.12
Medicines belonging to EMPLOYEES or to children in a day care center that
require refrigeration and are stored in a FOOD refrigerator shall be: (A)
Stored in a package or container and kept inside a covered, leakproof
container that is identified as a container for the storage of medicines; P
and (B) Located so they are inaccessible to children. P 7-208 First Aid
