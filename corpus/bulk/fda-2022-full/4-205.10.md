## 4-205.10
FOOD EQUIPMENT that is certified or classified for sanitation in conformance
to a recognized American National Standard by an American National Standards
Institute (ANSI)-accredited certification program is deemed to comply with
Parts 4-1 and 4-2 of this chapter. 4-3  Numbers and Capacities 4-301 Equipment
