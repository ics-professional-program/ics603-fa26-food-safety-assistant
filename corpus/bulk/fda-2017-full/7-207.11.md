## 7-207.11(A)
(A) Except for medicines that are stored or displayed for retail sale, only
those medicines that are necessary for the health of EMPLOYEES shall be
allowed in a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT. Pf

## 7-207.11(B)
(B) Medicines that are in a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT for the EMPLOYEES' use shall be
labeled as specified under § 7-101.11 and located to prevent the contamination
of FOOD, EQUIPMENT, UTENSILS, LINENS, and SINGLE-SERVICE and SINGLE-USE
PARTICLES.
