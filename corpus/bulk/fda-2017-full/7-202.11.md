## 7-202.11(A)
(A) Only those POISONOUS OR TOXIC MATERIALS that are required for the
operation and maintenance of a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT, such as for the cleaning
and SANITIZING of EQUIPMENT and
UTENSILS and the control of insects and rodents, shall be allowed in a FOOD
ESTABLISHMENT. Pf (B) Paragraph (A) of this section does not apply to PACKAGED
P OISONOUS OR TOXIC MATERIALS that are for retail sale.
