## 5-302.14
If provided, a water tank vent shall terminate in a downward direction and
shall be covered with: (A) 16 mesh to 25.4 mm (16 mesh to 1 inch) screen or
equivalent when the vent is in a protected area; or (B) A protective filter
when the vent is in an area that is not protected from windblown dirt and
debris.
