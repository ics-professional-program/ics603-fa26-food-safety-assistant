# FDA Food Code §3-401 — Cooking Temperatures

Adapted from the FDA Food Code 2022 edition, §3-401.11: minimum internal
cooking temperatures for raw animal foods. Teaching data, not current
guidance.

## Poultry and stuffed foods
Raw poultry, including whole or ground chicken, turkey, and duck, must be
cooked to a minimum internal temperature of 165°F (74°C), held for less
than one second (instantaneous). The same 165°F standard applies to stuffed
foods, stuffing that
contains raw meat, and any dish combining raw poultry with other ingredients.

## Ground meats
Ground or mechanically tenderized meats such as ground beef and ground pork
must reach a minimum internal temperature of 155°F (68°C) for 17 seconds.
This also applies to flaked, minced, and injected meats.

## Whole-muscle meats, seafood, and eggs
Seafood, whole-muscle steaks and chops of beef or pork, and raw shell eggs
cooked for immediate service must reach 145°F (63°C) for 15 seconds. Whole
beef or pork roasts may use alternative time-temperature combinations, such
as 130°F (54°C) held for 112 minutes.
