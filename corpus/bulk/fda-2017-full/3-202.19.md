## 3-202.19
When received by a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT, SHELLSTOCK shall be reasonably free of
mud, dead shellfish, and shellfish with broken shells.  Dead shellfish or
SHELLSTOCK with badly broken shells shall be discarded. Commercially Processed
