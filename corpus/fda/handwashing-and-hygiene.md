# FDA Food Code §2-301 — Handwashing and Personal Hygiene

Adapted from the FDA Food Code 2022 edition, §§2-301.12 and 3-301.11:
employee hygiene requirements. Teaching data, not current guidance.

## When to wash hands
Food employees must wash their hands before starting food preparation, after
handling raw meat or poultry, after using the restroom, after coughing or
sneezing, after touching the face or hair, after handling garbage, and after
any activity that contaminates the hands.

## How to wash hands
Wash in a designated handwashing sink using soap and warm running water for
at least 20 seconds total, including 10 to 15 seconds of vigorous scrubbing
of hands, fingers, and forearms. Dry with a single-use paper towel.

## Bare-hand contact
Bare-hand contact with ready-to-eat food is prohibited. Use single-use
gloves, deli tissue, tongs, or other utensils. Gloves must be changed
between tasks and are never a substitute for handwashing.
